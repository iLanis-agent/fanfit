Fan fit

A ceiling fan goes wrong at the store: a span that stirs one corner of the room, a downrod that puts the blades at forehead height, or one fan doing the work of three. Fan fit turns the room sides into a blade span with labeled coverage, turns the ceiling height into the drop, and turns the floor plan into the fan count and their spacing. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Span plan - the room sides plus labeled coverage become the blade span.
  • Drop plan - the ceiling minus the labeled body and blade line becomes the downrod.
  • Count plan - the floor area at the labeled coverage becomes the fans and their spacing.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: blade spans of 76, 107, 132 and 152 cm covering 7, 13, 23 and 37 m2, a 30 cm fan body, a 240 cm target blade line, and a 240 cm minimum ceiling - all illustrative defaults you can change by measuring. A real fan hangs from a fan-rated box and follows the maker's instructions and the local electrical code. This is sizing arithmetic, not a wiring ruling or a safety rating: measure the real room and let the maker's sheet govern.
